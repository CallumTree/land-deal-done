## 4. What makes it a poor comparison

1. **Count set by condition.** Condition 5 makes 20 the minimum (#1 p3). The count is a policy floor, not a capacity outcome.
2. **Mix outside the engine's types.** It has 5-bed detached and 1-bed flats. The engine's first-pass types are 2-bed terrace, 3-bed semi and 4-bed detached (CLAUDE.md).
3. **Site area not stated.** No document gives an area, so density (dph) can't be checked against a sourced figure.
4. **One approved layout is unseen.** 100-01 and the 20293/* engineering drawings were approved but aren't in the folder.
5. **Entrance is inconsistent across sources.** The report says access is off the A487 to the south west (#3 p1). The layout shows the entrance off the north-west road. OS names that road Hill Lane and names the A487 "Keeston Hill" (see section 5).
6. **Already built.** Development started 01/05/2023 (#4 p4). OS data for October 2026 shows estate roads and buildings on the site. That doesn't stop it being a hold-out, but the as-built layout may differ from 516-03 Rev B.
7. **Not checked: red line versus developed area.** The south-east field gate suggests access to land beyond the site. Whether the red line holds undeveloped land would need an overlay or trace, which this intake doesn't do.

## 5. Position check (no fixture, no shift applied)

**Method.** Location plan #7 rendered at 300 dpi. Scale 1:2500, confirmed by the plan's scale bar (4.77 px/m measured against 4.72 nominal). Fixed scale, north-up, translation only. The plan was registered by matching the midlines of its drawn carriageways to OS Open Roads centrelines for three segments: the A487 south-east of the junction, the A487 west of the junction, and Hill Lane. Two iterations; the final residual is 0.2 m or less per segment. OS Open Map-Local buildings were checked by eye only, because the generalised outlines didn't give a usable automatic fit. Data: OS Open Roads and OS Open Map-Local, both October 2026 release, tile SM.

**Results.**
- **Location plan versus OS.** After registration the plan's linework spans E 189159–189489, N 219134–219379. The plan states E 189158–189505, N 219118–219393. The west edge agrees to about 1 m; the other edges are clipped linework, so they can't be tested. Road-midline residuals are 0.2 m or less, with an interquartile spread of about ±1–2 m along each road. **Plan-to-OS offset: about 1 m. Confidence: high (±2–3 m).**
- **Form grid ref (E189309, N219321) versus the site.** It falls inside the registered red line, about 14 m from the red line's centre (roughly E189320, N219312). It is about 9 m from the OS estate access link. **Offset of the grid ref from the site centre: about 14 m. It is a general locating point inside the site, not a corner or the entrance.**
- **Entrance versus OS Open Roads.** OS shows the estate access joining **Hill Lane** at about E189276 N219341. That point is 0.7 m from the registered red line on its north-west frontage, which matches the 516-03 Rev B entrance off the north-west "HIGHWAY". OS names the A487 east of the junction "Keeston Hill" and the north-east road "Hill Lane".
- **As-built check.** OS Open Roads and Open Map-Local show estate links inside the red line: an access off Hill Lane to a node at about E189300 N219319, a "Restricted Local Access Road" north-east (consistent with the "PRIVATE ROAD" on 516-03 Rev B), and a "Local Road" south-east then north-east. No link reaches the south-east field gate. This is broadly the same road layout as 516-03 Rev B. It is not a dimensional check.
