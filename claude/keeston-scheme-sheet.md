## 2. Which layout is the approved one

**100-01 is not in either folder.** 19/0968/PA dates from 2020. 100-01 belongs to the 2025 DOC (100-series drawings are dated 31.03.25; D9–D13). Whether it differs from 516-03 Rev B **can't be determined from the files**.

| 516-03 Rev A, 31.01.20 | Replaced by Rev B. Rev A against Rev B: the only differences are the landscaping note text and the revision box ("REV B 6-03-20 LANDSCAPING NOTE AMENDED"). Text diff, plus a pixel diff confined to those two areas: layout geometry identical | R18 vs R8 |
| **516-03 Rev B, 6-03-20** | **Approved plan, condition 1 of 19/0968/PA** ("516 03 rev B Proposed site layout plan"). Approved again under 25/0117/DC for conditions 7 and 9 | R21 p1; D2 p1 |
| 100-01 (no revision letter) | Approved under 25/0117/DC alongside 516-03 Rev B. Not seen | D2 p1; D1 p3–4 |

**Ground truth: 516-03 Rev B.** It is the plan named in the reserved-matters approval (R21 p1, condition 1), and layout was a reserved matter. The 2025 DOC re-approved it for conditions 7 and 9 (D2 p1). The 2025 form calls it "approved site layout 516-03B" (D4 p4), and the highways parking assessment was made on it (D16 p1). 100-01 was approved alongside it under a discharge of conditions, not as a replacement. A discharge of conditions can't change a reserved-matters layout, which would need a new RM or NMA application. So 100-01 can't supersede 516-03 Rev B as the approved layout, although it may show the as-built road. Remaining risk: 100-01 not seen.

Note: the 19/0968/PA delegated report cites "drawing no. 515-03 rev A" for landscaping (R20 p4, para 8.11). The decision notice approves "516 03 rev B" (R21 p1). Read 515 as a typo for 516.

## 4. What makes it a poor comparison

1. **Count set by condition.** Condition 5 makes 20 the minimum (D1 p3). Density was tested against the 25 dph policy floor (R20 p3).
2. **Mix outside the engine's types.** 5-bed detached and 1-bed flats are outside the engine's three house types.
3. **No turning head.** It was deliberately omitted (R18/R8 revision note). The engine's Manual for Streets turning-head rule would not be satisfied on the real scheme.
4. **Built form count conflict.** The report says 12 detached and 21 units in total (R20 p1); the plans show 11 and 20.
5. **One approved drawing unseen.** 100-01 and the 20293/* engineering drawings are missing, so road widths come from layout labels only and footway and verge widths aren't known.
6. **Already built.** Started 01/05/2023. OS data for October 2026 shows the estate.

## 5. Position check and area measurement (no fixture, no shift applied)

**Position (round 1, unchanged).** D7 was registered to OS Open Roads at 1:2500, north-up, translation only. Road-midline residuals are 0.2 m or less. **Plan-to-OS offset about 1 m; confidence high (±2–3 m).** The DOC grid ref E189309 N219321 lies inside the red line, about 14 m from its centre. The RM grid ref E189321 N219317 (R1 p1) is about 12 m from the DOC grid ref and also inside. The estate access joins Hill Lane at about E189276 N219341, 0.7 m from the red line.

**Area (round 2, measured).**
- **Method.** D7 and R2 rendered at 300 dpi. The red outline was closed and its interior filled. Area = interior plus half the line pixels (centreline), converted at the 1:2500 nominal scale of 4.724 px/m.
- **Scale check.** The scale bar reads −10 to 50 m over 284 px, which is 4.73 px/m (+0.2% against nominal). Area depends only on scale, so the 1 m position offset has no effect.
- **Result.** D7 gives 7,201 m²; R2 gives 7,203 m². **Measured 0.720 ha.** Line-width band 0.698–0.742 ha; scale uncertainty ±0.5% or less.
- **Against the stated figure.** Stated 0.72 ha (R20 p3). Difference ≈ 0.
- This is a whole-polygon area from the red line only. No vertices were traced into any fixture.

## 7. Verdict (home count)

**Non-comparable.**
The area (0.72 ha stated and measured) and the count (20) are solid. But 9 of 20 homes are types the engine doesn't build (5-bed detached, 1-bed flats), the count is a condition floor, and the scheme deliberately omits a turning head the engine would require. The engine-against-real comparison isn't on fair footing; this follows the precedent of Meads, which was marked non-comparable because of its flats.
